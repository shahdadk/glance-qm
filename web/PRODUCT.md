# kompX

## Register

product

## Users

People listening, working, or meeting someone who need one useful thought without leaving the conversation. The actual Meta glasses app is the primary wearable surface. This browser is a simple companion for the same ongoing context and its tasks.

## Product purpose

Listen explicitly, show a stable useful cue, preserve evidence, and carry requested work forward. The main browser flow is Start → useful text → Tasks / Summary. Sessions are created or resumed internally; users do not enter room IDs or manage infrastructure to start a conversation. Connection pairing and diagnostic details belong in Settings, outside the everyday flow.

## Brand personality

Attentive, grounded, restrained. Use plain language and calm black-and-white controls. Do not perform intelligence, display invented activity, or use marketing copy to stand in for a working product.

## Anti-references

- Dashboards, provider status panels, visible room-ID forms, feature catalogs, and transcript columns in the primary experience.
- Marketing heroes, decorative glass cards, neon HUDs, gradients, or repeated icon-card grids.
- Fake presence, fabricated evidence, simulated provider connectivity, or browser controls described as a real Meta glasses connection.
- Silent external actions or confirmation detached from the exact proposal.

## Design principles

1. One useful thought at a time, stable enough to read.
2. Listening starts deliberately; capture state is honest.
3. Evidence and deeper detail are available without crowding the main flow.
4. Context, corrections, and follow-through stay in one continuous session.
5. Actions remain inspectable, and external sends require explicit confirmation of the exact recipients, content, and timing.
6. Keep full capabilities in details/settings rather than making users operate infrastructure.

## Accessibility & inclusion

Target WCAG AA: high contrast, 44 px targets, visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, reduced motion, text scaling, and a manual transcript fallback under Advanced when browser speech recognition is unavailable. Do not encode state in color alone.
